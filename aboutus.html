Some Information about Us

Welcome to Malnad Non-Veg Restaurant, where flavor meets tradition! We specialize in serving authentic non-vegetarian dishes made with fresh ingredients and carefully selected spices. From grilled chicken, tandoori delights, and mutton curry to seafood specialties, every dish is prepared to give you a rich and memorable dining experience. Our mission is to provide delicious food, warm hospitality, and a comfortable atmosphere for all our guests. Whether you’re dining in or ordering to-go, we promise quality, taste, and satisfaction in every bite. Visit us and discover the true taste of Malnad!
